About the data

Designed for coordination, planning and partner validation.

5Ws structure

Each operational row is organised around Who, What, Where, When and Why, with supporting funding, population and verification fields.

Public sources

Records draw on OCHA/HDX operational presence, historical 3W data and DRC population projections. Source links are retained in the downloadable workbook.

Use with care

Coordinates are approximate reference points, population figures are planning estimates, and entries marked for validation should be confirmed with partners before reporting.
