About the data

Designed for coordination, planning and partner validation.

5Ws structure

Each operational row is organised around Who, What, Where, When and Why, with supporting funding, population and verification fields.

Public sources

Records draw on OCHA/HDX operational presence, historical 3W data, DRC population projections and WorldPop 2024. Source links are retained in the downloadable workbook.

Use with care

Coordinates are approximate reference points. WorldPop values are scenario denominators and estimated beneficiaries must be validated with partners before reporting.

Methodology & validation

How the OWR Stakeholder Map is built and maintained.

1. Define the 5Ws

Each record identifies Who is operating, What activity is delivered, Where it takes place, When it operates, and Why the intervention is needed.

2. Compile public sources

Operational records are compiled from OCHA/HDX 3W and operational-presence datasets, historical 2018 3W records, DRC population projections and WorldPop 2024. Every imported record retains its source and period.

3. Standardise the data

Organisation names, sectors, territories, localities, dates, operation years, funders, budgets and population fields are normalised into the shared workbook model.

4. Check geography

Locality and territory references are matched to approximate coordinates. These are planning references, not facility-level GPS, and are flagged for field validation.

5. Validate with partners

Partners and clusters should confirm current presence, activity status, exact location, dates, beneficiaries, funding and budget before the data is used for formal reporting.

6. Estimate and publish

WorldPop locality population is calculated within a 5 km radius and multiplied by a 10% planning factor. The result is a scenario estimate, not a beneficiary count; validated changes retain an update date and source note.

Validation rule: population figures are planning denominators, not beneficiary totals; approximate map points are not facility coordinates; a data gap does not mean that no activity exists.
